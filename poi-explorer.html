POI map explorer

Dev-only visualization — ingest artifacts for scenic-router are unchanged (SCENIC-ROUTER-INGEST.md).

Serve from repo root (required — file:// breaks the map):

python3 -m http.server 8765

Then open http://localhost:8765/poi-explorer.html#amenities

Park amenities: search 02-state-arcgis, 03-provincial-osm, Parks Canada.

Rebuild data after pipeline runs:

node build-poi-explorer-data.mjs
